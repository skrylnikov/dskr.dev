On this trip, I walked around Kant Island, visited the zoo and Amalienau's old villas, and explored Fort No. 1 Stein with a flashlight at night. I also went to Svetlogorsk, the Curonian Spit and Zelenogradsk. These posts cover old houses, sand dunes, the Baltic, cats and the inner workings of a Prussian fort.
