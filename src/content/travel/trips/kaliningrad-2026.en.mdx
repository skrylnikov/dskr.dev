On this trip, I walked around Kant Island, visited the zoo and Amalienau's old villas, and explored Fort No. 1 Stein with a flashlight at night. I also went to Svetlogorsk, the Curonian Spit and Zelenogradsk, visited the ships at the Museum of the World Ocean and Planet Ocean, and explored Fort No. 11 Dönhoff. These posts cover old houses, sand dunes, the Baltic, cats, museum ships and the inner workings of Prussian forts.
